# FocusFlow — Product Features

An all-round productivity and time management app that helps people plan, prioritize, journal, and actually achieve their goals.

**Primary user:** Busy professionals balancing work, family, and personal ambitions.

---

## 1. Goals → Projects → Tasks

Every piece of work traces back to a goal, so users always see how today's effort connects to a bigger ambition.

- **Goals** — Outcome-level ambitions with a target date and progress bar. Users define 3 to 5 active goals at a time to keep focus sharp.
- **Projects** — Meaningful chunks of work that move a goal forward (e.g. "Launch Q3 presentation" under "Career growth").
- **Tasks** — Concrete actions, each with an estimated duration, difficulty, optional deadline, and a priority.
- **Goal progress view** — A simple, fast-to-read screen showing how far along each goal is, what's currently blocking it, and the next actions.
- **Goal health checks** — The app flags goals that have had no activity, warns when too many goals compete for attention, and prompts the user to cut or reschedule.

## 2. Smart Quick Capture

Capture must never interrupt the user's train of thought, wherever they are.

- **Voice capture** — Speak a thought and the app transcribes and files it as a task, note, or journal line.
- **Text capture** — Type naturally ("Call the dentist tomorrow at 3pm, urgent") and the app extracts the action, date, time, and priority.
- **Single inbox** — Everything captured lands in one place to be triaged later, with a daily prompt to clear it.
- **Ambiguity handling** — When the app is unsure, it asks one short clarifying question rather than guessing.

## 3. Prioritization

Users stop deciding what matters by gut feel alone.

- **Customizable frameworks** — Choose from Eisenhower (urgent/important), Impact vs Effort, or build your own criteria.
- **Automatic ranking** — The chosen framework scores every task and produces a clear order, with the reasoning shown.
- **Today's Top 3** — The morning plan highlights the three tasks that matter most, chosen from the framework and goal weights.
- **Deliberate elimination** — The app actively suggests what to drop, defer, or delegate rather than letting the list grow indefinitely.

## 4. Auto-Scheduled Day

The app builds the day so the user doesn't have to.

- **Energy-aware scheduling** — Each user defines their energy pattern; demanding, creative work is placed in sharp hours and admin or mechanical work in low-energy windows.
- **Deadline and priority weighting** — Urgent and high-impact work claims the best available slots first.
- **Working hours and boundaries** — Planning respects real working hours, commutes, and protected personal time.
- **Ambient awareness** — Focus sessions and recovery time are built into the day, not treated as optional extras.
- **Editable but not fiddly** — Users can drag, resize, or reject any block; the app absorbs changes and re-plans the rest.
- **Two-way calendar sync** — Meetings and appointments from Google, Outlook, or Apple Calendar block time automatically, so the plan never conflicts with reality.

## 5. The Daily Loop

A short ritual that keeps the user engaged and the system informed.

**Morning plan (5 minutes)**
- Confirm or adjust today's schedule
- See and lock in the Top 3
- Log current energy level with one tap

**Evening review (3–5 questions)**
- What got done today, and what didn't
- What worked and what got in the way
- Energy and mood for the day
- One win to acknowledge, one lesson to keep
- Optional journal entry, prompted by the coach and connected to the day's work

**Automatic replan with consent**
- Missed work is never punished
- The app proposes a revised plan, clearly showing what was cut, what was pushed, and why
- One tap to accept, or adjust before accepting

## 6. Focus Mode

Protects the time the scheduler has defended.

- **One-tap focus session** started from any scheduled task
- **Distraction blocking** — notifications silenced and distracting apps blocked for the session's duration
- **Smart break suggestions** — breaks that respect the user's energy curve rather than fixed intervals
- **Session outcome** — On finishing, log what was accomplished and whether the estimate was accurate, feeding future scheduling.

## 7. Routines

Recurring behavior lives inside the plan rather than in a separate system.

- Repeatable routines (e.g. "Morning review", "Weekly planning", "Gym") that auto-schedule like any other task
- Flexible cadence — daily, weekdays only, twice a week, or a custom pattern
- Consistency tracked per routine with gentle surfacing of slippage
- Routines can be paused without losing their history

## 8. Journaling

Reflection that is part of productivity, not a separate diary.

- **Day-linked entries** — Every journal entry is attached to the day it happened and can reference the tasks and goals involved.
- **Guided prompts** — Coach asks questions that surface friction, patterns, and wins.
- **Free-form writing** with mood and gratitude capture
- **Searchable history** — Users can find the moment they thought about a specific problem
- **Weekly continuity** — Days are grouped into weeks so patterns are visible over time

## 9. Progress, Insights and Motivation

Proof that effort is producing results.

- **Goal progress view** — Simple, clean, immediately scannable
- **Automatic insight feed** — Patterns the app surfaces without effort, such as "You finish 3x more when you plan the night before" or "Thursdays are your weakest day"
- **Weekly review and course correction** — Review what advanced, what's stalled, and adjust goals before effort is wasted
- **Consistency scoring** — Based on completed versus uncompleted tasks each week
- **Top 20 weekly leaderboard** — Ranks the 20 most consistent users of the week, with display names rather than real names and full privacy opt-out
- **Streaks** — For daily planning rituals and routines, with compassionate framing on breakage
- **Strengths and weaknesses** — Honest breakdown of where the user excels and where they consistently fall short

## 10. Proactive AI Coach

A thinking partner, not a chatbot waiting to be asked.

- **Morning briefing** — A short spoken or written summary of the day ahead and what deserves attention
- **Overload detection** — Flags days that are over-committed and recommends what to remove
- **Journal interrogation** — Asks probing follow-up questions that surface root causes and blind spots
- **Pattern recognition** — Connects behavior across weeks, such as which task types always slip
- **Course correction** — Recommends dropping or rewriting goals that no longer serve the user
- **Capture assistance** — Breaks vague ideas into concrete next actions
- **Alerts only when useful** — The coach speaks up when it has something to say, and stays quiet otherwise

## 11. Smart, Minimal Reminders

- **Task start** — A nudge when a scheduled block begins
- **Schedule drift** — A gentle alert when the day is falling behind
- **Ritual prompts** — Morning plan and evening review at the user's chosen time
- **User-controlled** — Every category adjustable, snoozable, or silenced
- **Respectful by default** — No notification spam, no guilt language

## 12. Guided Onboarding

Short, structured, and ends with a real plan.

1. Set top 3 goals with target dates
2. Define working hours, boundaries, and energy pattern
3. Choose a prioritization framework
4. Connect calendar
5. App generates the first week automatically

Ends in under ten minutes with a usable plan rather than a blank canvas.

---

## The Core Loop

```
Set goals → Capture everything → App prioritizes → App builds the day
    ↑                                                        ↓
Coach corrects ← Weekly review ← Daily review ← Focus & adapt
```

## What Makes This Different

- **Energy-aware scheduling** instead of a flat to-do list
- **Goals are the spine** — every task justifies its existence
- **Reflection is built into the workflow**, not a separate journal app
- **Forgiving by design** — replanning is expected, never punished
- **Data-driven motivation** — consistency scoring and honest strength/weakness feedback instead of empty badges
